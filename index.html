how to read these

one device, held site-wide

Every essay carries a correspondence table: the neuroscience concept, the machine-learning concept, and a fidelity rating that is one of three words.

And every essay carries a where the mapping breaks section. Nothing here claims a correspondence without also stating its limit. If a claim survives that section, it has earned the space.

  • mechanism the same causal process, not only the same shape
  • analogy a structural match that predicts, but through different machinery
  • metaphor a useful figure of speech, load-bearing only for intuition
